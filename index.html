How it works

Three steps. No setup, no app-by-app rules to maintain.

1

Tell it your goal

One field: "what are you working on?" Everything is judged against that — your words, not a fixed list somebody else wrote.

2

It watches, quietly

The character sits small in the corner. Simple rules judge most windows instantly; for the unclear ones, an AI checks the page against your goal.

3

One honest intervention

Stay drifted for 10 seconds and the screen darkens, video pauses, sound mutes, and a card names the exact thing pulling you away. Two honest ways out.

Why not just a blocker

Blockers are a wall. This is a person who talks back.

Blockers remove the choice — which is exactly why they don't last. Your motivated self installs one; your distracted self turns it off five minutes later.

A blocker

  • A fixed list of "bad" apps someone else wrote
  • Silent until you fight past it, or just disable it
  • No idea what you're actually trying to do today
  • Feels like a wall — so you find the door

Focus Buddy

  • Judged against the goal you stated for THIS session
  • Stays quiet while you're on track, and steps in only when you stay drifted
  • Names the exact thing pulling you away, out loud
  • Always an honest way out: get back to work, or type why you're staying
Under the hood

Built to be honest about what it's doing, and gentle about how it does it.

Judged against your goal

Not a universal app list — your own stated intent for this session.

Staying takes a reason

"Yeah, I need this" asks you to type why. A moment to decide, not a reflex click.

One honest escalation

No ladder of nagging notifications — a soft edge-warning, then one clear intervention.

Pauses video, mutes sound

The distraction actually stops — not just gets covered up by a dark screen.

Never a trap

Your keyboard is never blocked. Every intervention has an immediate, honest way out.

Privacy by design

Window titles and URLs are never saved. For unclear windows, the AI sees only your goal, the app, the window title and the site name. Never the full URL, and nothing is logged.

Back to the exact tab

"Back to work" returns you to the app and the browser tab you were on, in Chrome, Safari and Edge.

See your week

A local dashboard shows each day and hour split into productive, neutral and distracted time.

Mac and Windows

Voice, video pause, muting and tab switching all work natively on both.

No screen recording

On a Mac it never asks for Screen Recording or Accessibility. It only checks which app is in front, and asks once per browser to read the current tab.

System requirements & first launch

Mac

  • macOS 13 Ventura or later
  • To install, open the .dmg and drag Focus Buddy to Applications
  • Apple Silicon (M1 or newer)
  • No Screen Recording or Accessibility permission needed
  • Asks once per browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Brave) to read the current tab, after explaining why
  • Beta builds aren't signed yet: if macOS blocks it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway

Windows

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • No extra permissions needed
  • Beta builds aren't signed yet: if SmartScreen warns you, click More info → Run anyway

Both: an internet connection for AI judgment of unclear windows. Without one, it falls back to its built-in rules.
